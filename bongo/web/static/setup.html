    Trying …

    Your phone will lose the setup network now. That's expected.

    If it works, the panel shows its new address and a code. Once your phone is back on your own Wi-Fi, scan the code to open the dashboard.

    If it doesn't, setup comes back in about a minute: join again (same password) and this page will say what went wrong.

    Joined

    Put your phone back on that Wi-Fi, then scan the code on the panel to open the dashboard.

    Having trouble?
    • This Matrix only does 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi (a Pi Zero can't see 5 GHz). If your router gives each band its own name, pick the 2.4 GHz one. If it only has one name, check the router's settings that 2.4 GHz is switched on.
    • The setup page didn't open by itself? While joined to the setup network, open 10.42.0.1 in your browser. The panel shows it too.
    • Passwords are case-sensitive. Tap Show to check it. Some routers give 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz different names: check you picked the right one.
    • Hidden network? Tap My network isn't listed, type its exact name and tick hidden.
    • Wi-Fi with a sign-in page (hotels, some guest networks) and work or school Wi-Fi that asks for a username won't work.
    • Too far from the router? Try it closer first; it remembers the network when you move it back.
    • Setup closes after 15 minutes if nobody uses it, and comes back after 5 minutes without Wi-Fi (or from Settings > Wi-Fi > Reopen setup).
